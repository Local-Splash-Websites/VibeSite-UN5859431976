About TrueBuilt Construction Group, LLC

A fully insured, Rochester-based remodeling contractor helping homeowners improve, update, and expand their homes since 2021.

Our Story

Dedicated to quality and lasting results

TrueBuilt Construction Group, LLC helps homeowners improve, update, and expand their homes. We are fully insured and focused on quality workmanship, on-time delivery, and solutions tailored to each project. Our promise is simple: Built to Last.

We handle kitchen and bathroom remodeling, basement and whole-home remodeling, additions, decks, fencing, garages, and emergency work. Because our team is in-house, you work with one contractor from the first conversation to the final walkthrough.

Our Mission

Building spaces that inspire confidence

Our mission is to restore trust in the remodeling process by delivering uncompromising quality, on-time delivery, and personalized solutions tailored to each client's vision.
