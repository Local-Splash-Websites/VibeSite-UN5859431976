Home Restoration

Restore worn spaces with care

Home restoration returns worn, damaged, or aging areas to a safe, useful condition, covering surface repairs, damaged areas, worn materials, and overall property condition. Addressing small problems early protects property value and keeps walls, floors, ceilings, and trim looking cared for. We serve Rochester, NY and the surrounding region.
