Our Story 

Dedicated To Quality And Lasting Results 

When your home needs an upgrade, you want a remodeling contractor who can turn your ideas into lasting, well-built results. At TrueBuilt Construction Group LLC in Rochester, NY, we help residential customers improve, update, and expand their homes with skilled craftsmanship and personalized service. We are fully insured and committed to restoring trust in the remodeling process through uncompromising quality, on-time delivery, and solutions tailored to each client’s vision. Our promise, “Built to Last,” reflects the standard we bring to every project.

We handle home remodeling projects of all sizes with an in-house team that keeps each project organized from start to finish. As a home renovation contractor and general remodeling contractor, we provide residential remodeling for kitchens, bathrooms, basements, living spaces, and more. Our kitchen remodeling and kitchen renovation services improve layouts, finishes, storage, and function, while bathroom remodeling and bathroom renovation can create more comfortable and practical spaces. We also provide basement remodeling, interior remodeling, exterior remodeling, whole home remodeling, custom home remodeling, and garage remodeling. Whether you need a house remodeling contractor for a major transformation, a room addition contractor for extra space, or a home addition contractor for a larger expansion, our remodeling company focuses on quality workmanship and attention to detail. Our services also include additions, decks, fencing, and deck remodeling to help make your property more functional, attractive, and enjoyable. Because we keep everything in-house, you can work with one contractor throughout the project instead of coordinating multiple crews. We also offer emergency services and, when scheduling and project conditions allow, sometimes provide same-day service.

Since 2021, we have built our approach around dependable scheduling, tailored solutions, and workmanship designed for long-term value. If you are planning a renovation, addition, repair, or complete residential upgrade, contact TrueBuilt Construction Group LLC in Rochester, NY. Discover how we can bring your vision to life with results that are built to last.
